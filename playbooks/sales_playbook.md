# CRM
## Pipeline stages
- **New**: lead captured, not yet contacted. Must be contacted within 2 business days.
- **Qualified**: budget, decision-maker and timeline confirmed (BANT). Max 14 days in stage.
- **Proposition**: quote or proposal sent. Follow up every 5 business days. Max 21 days in stage.
- **Won**: signed order. Hand over to onboarding within 1 business day.

## Qualification rules
A lead becomes an opportunity only when all of these are true:
1. Expected revenue is at least 5,000.
2. We have spoken with someone who can approve the purchase.
3. The customer has a target decision date within 6 months.

## Discount policy
- Up to 10%: salesperson can approve.
- 10–20%: sales manager approval required.
- Above 20%: VP Sales approval required; log the reason in the opportunity chatter.

## Stalled opportunities
An opportunity is "stalled" when it has exceeded the maximum days for its stage
or has no scheduled activity. Stalled opportunities are reviewed every Monday.
